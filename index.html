About Us

Professional legal assistance with a disciplined, client-focused approach.

Abro Law Chamber is based in Mini Bypass, Dera Murad Jamali, Balochistan, Pakistan. The chamber provides legal representation in criminal, civil, bail, narcotics, service, and regulatory matters, with attention to factual review, procedural compliance, and sound legal strategy.

Every case is assessed on its individual facts and applicable law. The chamber does not make guarantees regarding outcomes, and legal services are tailored to the specific circumstances of each matter.

Recent legal matters handled by the chamber.

Criminal Defence

State v. Shah Muhammad

  • Practice Area: Criminal Defence
  • Legal Provisions: Sections 324 and 34 PPC
  • FIR Date: 19 August 2026
  • Bail Date: 10 September 2026
  • Location: Hazara Town Police Station, Quetta

According to the information provided to the chamber, the complainant alleged that the accused fired a gunshot at her leg and that she was later taken in a car to Sajawal Abad. The matter was registered under Sections 324 and 34 PPC. The defence subsequently pursued bail after arrest.

Outcome: Bail After Arrest Obtained. This statement is subject to verification of the relevant court order.

Financial & Regulatory

Muhammad Ali Waqas — Bank Account Defreezing Matter

  • Practice Area: Financial and Regulatory Legal Assistance
  • Agency: Federal Investigation Agency, Islamabad
  • Inquiry Reference: No. 250/2025 (subject to verification)
  • Freezing Date: 23 November 2025
