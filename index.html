State v. Shah Muhammad

Practice Area: Criminal Defence  |  Legal Provisions: Sections 324 & 34 PPC

FIR Registration Date: 19 August 2026  |  Bail After Arrest: 10 September 2026

Brief factual background

According to information provided to the chamber, the complainant (reported to be a former wife) alleged that the accused fired a gunshot at her leg and that she was transported in a vehicle to Sajawal Abad. The FIR was registered at Hazara Town Police Station, Quetta. These facts are published here as provided by the client and are marked for verification.

Legal assistance provided

The chamber provided legal representation in pursuing bail after arrest in proceedings arising from allegations under Sections 324 and 34 of the Pakistan Penal Code. Representation included case assessment, drafting of bail application, and court attendance as required.

Verified procedural outcome

Outcome: Bail After Arrest Obtained. (Subject to verification of the court order.)

Verification required before wider publication: FIR copy, court bail order, court name, and identity of counsel who represented the client in court.

2. Bail After Arrest and Pre-Arrest Bail

Assistance with bail after arrest and pre-arrest bail applications, including drafting, filing, and court attendance. Outcomes depend on case facts and judicial discretion.

3. Civil Litigation and Property Disputes

Representation in civil suits, property matters, injunctions, and related court procedures.

4. Control of Narcotic Substances (CNS) Cases

Legal defence and representation in matters under the Control of Narcotic Substances Act and related regulations.

5. Service Matters and Service Tribunal Litigation

Representation before service tribunals and administrative bodies in employment and service-related disputes.

6. Legal Drafting and Court Applications

Preparation of petitions, applications, notices, and other court documents, delivered with careful legal research and compliance with procedural rules.

7. Financial and Regulatory Legal Assistance

Assistance in banking, regulatory inquiries, and liaison with investigative agencies. We do not claim that assistance will close an inquiry unless documentary evidence confirms that result.

8. Legal Consultation and Case Assessment

Initial case assessments, legal strategy sessions, and ongoing advisory support tailored to client needs.
